How it works
Three steps to your AI agent

No servers, no code, no monthly subscription for hosting. Just your customised AI chatbot, live on the internet.

01
🎨
Pick & customise
Choose a template that fits your industry. Add your company name, logo emoji, brand colours, and a persona for your AI agent.
02
📚
Feed your knowledge
Paste your FAQs, upload product manuals, add URLs from your help centre. The AI learns your business and answers based on your content.
03
🚀
Deploy free
Download your customised HTML file. Upload to GitHub Pages — free hosting, your own URL. Share it with your team or embed it on your site.
04

Unresolved queries auto-generate support tickets. Resolved sessions improve the knowledge base over time — the bot gets smarter every week.
