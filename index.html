Before you deploy
Know your AI employee

An AI assistant is a genuinely useful hire — but it's still a new kind of hire. Here's exactly what it can and can't do, so you deploy it with confidence, not crossed fingers.

📚
It only knows what you teach it
Your chatbot answers from the knowledge base and instructions you give it — nothing more. No content, no confident guessing about your business.
🤝
It hands off when it's unsure
Every template includes an escalation message so tricky or sensitive questions get routed to a real person instead of the AI guessing.
🔎
Review it before customers see it
Use the free demo mode and live preview to test real questions your customers would ask, before it ever goes live on your site.
🔌
You're always in control
It's a file you own, on hosting you control. Take it down, edit its instructions, or turn off the backend at any moment — no contract, no lock-in.

Like any new team member, give it clear instructions and check its early answers. It's a tool to handle the repetitive questions so you can focus on the ones that need a human touch — not a replacement for your judgement on anything sensitive, legal, medical, or financial.

Common questions
Before you get started

Answers to what everyone asks the first time.

Do I need a website already?
No. GitHub Pages gives your chatbot its own free web address on its own. If you already have a website, you can drop it in with a one-line embed code from the setup guide — but it works perfectly well on its own too.
What if I get stuck or mess up the setup?
Every step has a plain-English guide with click-by-click instructions and a troubleshooting section for the most common mistakes. Nothing you do can break your computer or cost you money by accident — worst case, you delete the files and start again.
What if my chatbot says something wrong to a customer?
Test it in demo mode first with real questions your customers ask. Every template includes a built-in hand-off message for anything it's unsure about, and you can edit or turn it off at any time — it only ever answers from the information you give it.
Is my customers' data safe?
Conversations go straight from your chatbot to your own private backend to Anthropic's Claude API and back — nothing passes through DIYChatbots' servers, because we don't have any. You control the backend, so you control where the data goes.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. Every step is point-and-click — creating accounts, pasting a file, ticking a couple of settings. You will be copying and pasting a few things exactly as shown, which is the most "technical" part of the whole process.
Can I cancel or remove it later?
Yes, any time. There's no subscription to DIYChatbots to cancel — you can delete the GitHub repository, pause the backend, or simply stop paying for AI usage, and the chatbot stops working immediately.
